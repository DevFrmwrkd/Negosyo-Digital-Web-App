Good to know

Often asked

How do I book a room?

Tap any contact button on this page. It opens a direct chat with me — send the room, your dates and your headcount, and I will confirm what is free.

Do I pay through this site?

No — there is no checkout here. This page is for enquiries only. Once I confirm the room is free, we arrange payment between us directly.

Can I rent just one room?

Yes, that is the normal way. Each of the three rooms is let on its own, with its own key. You would be sharing the kitchen and terrace with at most two other rooms.

Can I take the whole house?

Yes. All three rooms together sleeps seven and gives you the kitchen, garden and terrace to yourselves. Two-night minimum on weekends.

Is the price on the card final?

That is the nightly rate for the room. The only thing added is a one-off cleaning fee, and I state it in writing when I reply.

What if I need to cancel?

Free cancellation up to five days before check-in. Inside five days, the first night is charged. I put the terms in writing before anything is agreed.
