The family record

Three generations, one counter

Ferreteria Reyes is a family-run hardware and construction supply store on Aguinaldo Highway, Bacoor, Cavite, stocking everything from fasteners and hand tools to cement, steel, and roofing. It serves homeowners mid-repair, maintenance crews, and contractors building whole subdivisions.

Lolo Reyes opened the store when Aguinaldo Highway was two lanes and a carabao lane. His rule was simple: never let a customer leave without the thing that finishes their job — even if it means opening a box to sell one bolt. That rule survived him, two highway widenings, and the arrival of three big-box competitors.

The third generation runs it now, with the same handwritten ledger habits — just backed by a computerized inventory of a few thousand line items.

Reader inquiries

Often asked at the counter

Do you deliver construction materials?

Yes — we deliver within Bacoor, Imus, Las Piñas, and Dasmariñas using our own trucks. Message your list and address for a delivery schedule, usually same or next day.

Can I send my materials list for canvassing?

Yes, that's how most contractors work with us. Send your bill of materials via Messenger and we return a complete canvass, usually within the day.

Do you cut wood, pipes, and glass to size?

Yes — plywood, lumber, GI pipes, and glass are cut to your measurements in-store while you wait, or prepared ahead for pickup.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Yes, unused items in original packaging can be exchanged or refunded within seven days with your receipt. Cut-to-size and special-order items are excluded.

Do you serve contractors and bulk orders?

Yes — contractors get a dedicated counter, canvass-by-Messenger, scheduled truck deliveries, and charge-account terms for repeat clients.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, GCash, Maya, bank transfer, and major cards. Contractor charge accounts are available after your third completed order.
