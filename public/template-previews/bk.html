The villa

Built for the sea, not for a brochure.

My grandfather cleared this slope in 1978 and the house went up around the two acacias that are still in the courtyard. I rebuilt it in 2021 — deeper eaves, a longer pool, better plumbing — without touching the footprint or the trees.

It is a house rather than a resort: 4 bedrooms, one long terrace, and a path down to water that almost nobody else uses. Staff arrive in the morning and are gone by dusk, so evenings are entirely yours.

Good to know

Often asked

How do I book the villa?

Tap any contact button on this page — it opens a direct chat with me. Send your dates and headcount and I will confirm availability with the full price written out.

Do I pay through this site?

No. There is no checkout here; this page is for enquiries only. Once we agree the dates and price, we arrange payment directly between us.

Can I rent a single room?

No — the villa is let whole, one booking at a time. That is what keeps the pool, terrace and cove path private for the group that is staying.

Is the cook really included?

Yes. Ana cooks breakfast daily plus one main meal, and her time is in the rate. You only cover what she buys at the market, at receipt.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to fourteen days before arrival. Inside fourteen days the first night is charged. The terms go in writing before anything is agreed.

Is it suitable in the rainy season?

June to October is greener and much cheaper, with rain that usually passes in an hour. I will tell you honestly what the sea is doing in the week you are asking about.
