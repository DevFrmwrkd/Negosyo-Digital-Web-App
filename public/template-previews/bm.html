Our story

A café for the street it sits on.

We opened in 2020 in a converted front room, mostly because the street had nowhere to sit down. Six tables then, nine now, and the same two of us behind the counter.

The coffee is from four Philippine farms and the kitchen is small on purpose — eggs, rice plates and whatever the bakery two streets over sends over that morning.

Good to know

Often asked

Do you take reservations?

Not for tables — seating is first come. For a group of six or more, message us and we will try to hold space.

Can I order ahead?

Yes, and it is the quickest way past the morning queue. Tap any contact button, send your order and a pickup time.

Do I pay through this page?

No. There is no checkout here; this page is for orders and enquiries only. You pay at the counter or on delivery.

Is there a time limit if I work here?

None, and no minimum spend. We only ask that you free up the bigger tables at lunch if you are on your own.

Do you have non-dairy milk?

Oat and soy, both at no extra charge. Just say so when you order.

Can I buy the beans you serve?

Everything on the board is on the retail shelf too, whole bean or ground to your brewer, roast date on the bag.
