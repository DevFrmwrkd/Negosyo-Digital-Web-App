The story

One grill, one family, one rule

Apoy at Asin is a family-run charcoal grill house in Concepcion Uno, Marikina serving inihaw na liempo, chicken inasal, and sinugba seafood to neighborhood families and barkadas. It's for anyone who wants proper uling-grilled food without leaving the barangay.

The first "branch" was a half-drum grill outside the family gate. Neighbors kept coming back, tables kept multiplying, and eventually the garage gave up and became a dining room. The rule from day one never changed: fire and salt do the work — no shortcuts, no gas grill, no pre-cooked anything.

Today the second generation runs the tongs. Same marinade written in a notebook nobody is allowed to photograph, same suppliers from Marikina Public Market every morning.

Off the grill

What we do over fire

Inihaw na liempo

Thick-cut pork belly, scored and basted until the edges char and the fat turns to glass.

Chicken inasal

Annatto-and-lemongrass marinated leg quarters, brushed with atsuete oil over live coals.

Sinugba seafood

Panga ng tuna, pusit, and tilapia straight from the morning market run, grilled in banana leaf.

Sabaw & gulay

Sinigang na baboy, pinakbet, and ensaladang talong — because somebody in the group always asks.

Party trays

Mixed ihaw platters for 10 to 150 heads, packed for pickup or delivered within Marikina.

Silog mornings

Weekend breakfast service: tapsilog, longsilog, and leftover-liempo silog for the early crowd.

Questions

Before you head over

Do you accept orders through Messenger?

Yes — Messenger is the fastest way to order. Send your order and pickup time and we confirm within minutes during service hours.

Is there parking at the restaurant?

Yes, we have slots for about six cars in front, plus street parking along Bayan-Bayanan Avenue after 6 PM.

Do you cater for parties and office events?

We do — grilled platters and party trays for 10 to 150 heads, delivered within Marikina and nearby Quezon City barangays. Message us at least two days ahead.

Can I reserve a table for a large group?

Yes, groups of six or more can reserve through Messenger or by phone. Weekends fill up fast, so book a day in advance.

Do you have non-grilled and vegetable dishes?

Yes — sinigang na baboy, ensaladang talong, pinakbet, and daily gulay round out the ihaw menu.

Is Apoy at Asin open on holidays?

We are open most holidays; hours can change, so check our Facebook page or Google listing before heading over.
