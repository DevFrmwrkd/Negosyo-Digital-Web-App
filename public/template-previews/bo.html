Our story

Real tea, real milk, pearls cooked hourly.

We started in 2021 with one shaker and a rice cooker full of pearls, selling to students on Esteban Abada. Same street now, bigger bar, same rule: nothing sits around.

Tea gets brewed fresh every two hours and anything past that goes down the sink. Pearls are cooked hourly, so they are still chewy at 9pm rather than turning to stone.

Good to know

Asked a lot

Can I really pick zero sugar?

Yes, on every drink including the brown sugar ones. It tastes different but plenty of regulars order it that way, so we are used to it.

How do I order ahead?

Tap any contact button — it opens a direct chat. Send your drinks, sugar and ice levels, and a pickup time. Ten minutes is usually enough.

Do I pay through this page?

No, there is no checkout here. This page is for orders and enquiries only; you pay at the counter or on delivery.

Are the pearls made fresh?

Cooked hourly in small batches and thrown out after two hours. That is the whole reason they are still chewy late at night.

Do you have dairy-free options?

The whole fruit tea line is dairy free, and any milk drink can be made with oat milk for a small charge.

Can you do bulk orders?

Yes — classes, offices and parties. Message us at least two hours ahead and we will have them boxed and labelled.
