our story, in the margin

Started with one whiteboard and a stubborn idea

Talino Review House is a tutoring and college-entrance review center in Obrero, Davao City for students from Grade 4 through senior high. It's for families who want small classes, honest progress reports, and teaching that starts from what the student actually knows.

The center began as weekend math tutorials in a borrowed classroom. The founding teacher noticed the same thing every session: students weren't slow — they had gaps, sometimes three grade levels down, that nobody had ever mapped. So she built a center where every enrollment starts with a diagnostic, and every lesson starts from the gap.

Ten years on, the method hasn't changed. The whiteboard collection, however, has grown considerably.

Diagnostic-first, always

Every student starts with a free 45-minute assessment. We teach the gap, not the syllabus.

Questions, answered

How do I enroll my child?

Message us on Messenger or visit the center to book a free diagnostic assessment. After the assessment we recommend a program, and you enroll at the desk or online.

What is the diagnostic assessment?

A free 45-minute test that maps your child's strengths and gaps by subject. Every program starts from it — we teach what your child needs, not a fixed syllabus.

How big are the classes?

Small by rule: a maximum of eight students per group class, and one-on-one for tutorial programs. Nobody hides in the back row here.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes — every program can run onsite, online, or blended. Online sessions use the same tutors, materials, and progress reports as onsite classes.

Which entrance exams do you review for?

We run review programs for the UPCAT, ACET, DCAT, USTET and other college entrance tests, plus NMAT foundations for pre-med students.

How do parents track progress?

Every four sessions you receive a written progress report with scores, topics covered, and what comes next — plus a short call with the tutor each month.

Who are the tutors?

Licensed teachers and honor graduates of Davao universities, each subject-matched and trained in our diagnostic-first method before handling a class.
