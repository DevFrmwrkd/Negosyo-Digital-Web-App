Our story

One roaster, six farms, no rush.

We opened in 2019 with one machine, four tables and beans from a single farm in Sagada. The room has not grown much since, but the list has: six farms now, all of them ones we have actually visited.

The roaster sits behind the bar, which means the coffee you drink was almost certainly roasted within the fortnight — and if you ask, whoever is on shift can tell you exactly when.

Good to know

Often asked

Do you take reservations?

Not for tables — seating is first come. For the long table or a group of six or more, message us and we will do our best to hold space.

Can I order ahead?

Yes, and it is the fastest way past the morning queue. Tap any contact button, send your order and a pickup time, and we will have it ready on the bar.

Is there a time limit if I work here?

No limit and no minimum spend. We only ask that you free up the four-seat tables at lunchtime if you are on your own.

Do you have non-dairy milk?

Oat, almond and soy, all at no extra charge. Say the word when you order.

Can I buy the beans you serve?

Every coffee on the board is on the retail shelf too, whole bean or ground to your brewer, with the roast date written on the bag.

Are you dog friendly?

On the outdoor tables, yes, and there is a water bowl by the door. Inside is kept for service animals only.

See you in the morning

Save yourself the queue.

Message us your order and a pickup time, or just walk in — there is almost always a table by the window.
