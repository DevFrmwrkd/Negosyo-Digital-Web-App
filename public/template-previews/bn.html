01 / The works

A workshop
that happens
to serve coffee

We took a ground-floor unit on Escolta in 2019 because it had the ceiling height for a roaster and a roller door wide enough to get it through.

The drum sits four metres from the bar. Roasting runs Tuesday and Friday mornings, which means the coffee in your cup was almost certainly roasted this week — and whoever hands it to you can tell you the exact day.

03 / Why here
01

Roast date, not best-before

Every bag is stamped with the day it came out of the drum. If it is older than three weeks we take it off the shelf rather than discount it.

02

Two farms, both named

We buy green directly from growers in Benguet and Sagada and visit each harvest. Ask where your cup is from and you get a farm and an altitude.

03

The person pouring roasted it

Small enough that the barista on shift knows the roast curve for what is in the hopper, and can tell you why it tastes the way it does.

10 / Notes

Asked at the bar

N01

Do you take reservations?

No — six seats, first come. Message us if you want to know how busy it is before you make the trip.

N02

Can I order ahead?

Yes, and it is the fastest route past the morning rush. Tap any contact button, send the order and a pickup time.

N03

Do I pay through this page?

No. There is no checkout here; this page is for orders and enquiries only. You pay at the bar or on delivery.

N04

Can I buy the beans you serve?

Everything on the list is on the retail shelf, whole bean or ground to your brewer, with the roast date stamped on the bag.

N05

Is it a place to work all day?

Honestly, not really — six seats and no power outlets on the bar. There is wifi, but we would rather you came for the coffee.

N06

Do you supply offices?

Yes, on a monthly subscription — beans, brewed batches or both. Message us with your headcount and we will work out a schedule.
