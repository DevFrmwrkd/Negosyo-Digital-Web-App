SEC 01

The shop

Labaná is a laundry and pressing shop in Kapasigan, Pasig — for busy households and small businesses that would rather spend their weekend on anything but a washing machine.

We started as a single row of machines and a hand iron, run by a family that took the "your clothes, our care" part seriously. Every order still gets a numbered ticket, its own load, and a check before it's folded — no mixing, no mystery socks.

Drop off at the counter or book a pickup and we'll bring it back the next day, folded and pressed. Simple, honest, and on time.

SEC 07

Questions

Do you pick up and deliver? +
Yes. Message or call to schedule a pickup within our Pasig service area; we collect your bag, process it and return it folded to your door. Pickup and delivery is part of the standard wash-dry-fold service.
What's the usual turnaround? +
Standard wash-dry-fold is typically ready the next day, and same-day is available if you drop off early. Pressing and dry cleaning may take a little longer; we confirm the ready time when you book.
How do you avoid mixing up laundry? +
Every order gets a numbered ticket and your bag is tagged to it. Loads are washed separately by order, never combined with other customers', and checked against the ticket before folding.
Can you handle delicates and special items? +
We do — barong, uniforms, bedding, curtains and delicate fabrics get the appropriate wash or dry-clean and press. Flag anything special when you drop off or book and we'll treat it accordingly.
Do you use gentle detergents? +
Yes. We use skin-friendly, low-residue detergents by default and can go fragrance-free or hypoallergenic on request — just note it on your ticket.
Do you take commercial or bulk laundry? +
We do regular laundry for cafés, salons, clinics and small offices — towels, linens and uniforms on a schedule. Message us about a standing pickup arrangement.
