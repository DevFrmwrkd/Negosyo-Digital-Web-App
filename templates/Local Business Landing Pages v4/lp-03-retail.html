01

Our store

Domingo Hardware & Supply is a family-run, full-line hardware store on C.M. Recto Avenue in Lipa City — the place tradespeople and homeowners come when they need the right part and they need it today.

The store started as a small bin of nails and galvanised wire out front of the family home, and grew one shelf at a time into six proper departments. Same family, same counter, deeper shelves.

We stock for the real job, not the catalogue photo: multiple sizes and grades of the things you actually run out of, and staff who've handed the same items across the counter long enough to know what pairs with what.

08

Questions

Do you mix paint to a colour I bring in? +
Yes. We tint and mix paint in-store to standard fan-deck colours and can match many samples you bring to the counter. Bring the chip or the item and our staff will sort it.
Can you cut wood, glass or keys? +
We cut plywood and board to size, cut glass to measure, and duplicate most house and padlock keys while you wait at the counter.
Do you deliver, and how far? +
We deliver within Lipa City and to nearby Batangas towns. Message us your list and location and we'll confirm delivery and load-out for bulk or heavy orders.
Do you offer accounts for contractors? +
We keep trade accounts for regular contractors and site orders, with consolidated pick-lists and delivery. Ask at the counter to set one up.
What if you don't stock what I need? +
Tell us the item — we carry a deep range across six departments and can often order in what isn't on the shelf. Message a photo and we'll check stock before you travel.
Is there parking and load-out? +
There is frontage parking on C.M. Recto Ave and a load-out bay at the side for heavy and bulk pickups. Staff can help you load.
