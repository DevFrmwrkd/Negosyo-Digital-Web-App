The studio

Héla is a small hair studio in Poblacion, Makati for people who want a cut and colour that actually suits them — not whatever's trending this week.

We keep the room deliberately small: one chair working at a time, one stylist with you from consultation to blow-dry. That means we can take the extra ten minutes on a fringe or a toning, and you're never handed off halfway through.

Our thing is lived-in colour and cuts built around how your hair actually behaves — so the look holds its shape as it grows out, and you spend less time fighting it in the mirror at home.

Before you book

Good to know

How do I book an appointment? +
Book through Messenger or by phone. Colour services begin with a short consultation, which you can request in the same message.
Do I need a consultation before colour? +
Yes for major colour changes and first-time balayage. It lets us assess your hair, agree on a realistic result and confirm the time and upkeep involved before you commit.
How far ahead should I book? +
A few days ahead is comfortable for cuts. For colour and weekend chairs, book about a week out; bridal and occasion styling should be arranged well in advance.
Do you style hair for weddings and events? +
We do. Bridal and occasion styling is by appointment and can include a trial run beforehand. Message us with your date to check availability.
Can I bring reference photos? +
Please do. References help us understand the look you want, and the consultation is where we translate them to what suits your hair and face.
Is there parking or transit nearby? +
Paid parking is available along Don Pedro St and in nearby lots. The studio is a short walk from the main Poblacion drop-off points.
