Our story

Kalan is an all-day Filipino kitchen and coffee house in Marikina, cooking the lutong-bahay dishes a Filipino family eats on an ordinary Tuesday — for anyone who walks in hungry. It began as one grandmother's carinderia and a clay kalan that never quite went cold.

The recipes haven't moved far from that first stove. We still simmer the adobo down slow, still toast pandesal over live coals, still brew barako by the pot instead of the cup. What changed is the room around it — a little brighter, a little longer, with space to sit and stay.

Come for almusál before work, merienda in the afternoon, or a long unhurried dinner. There is always kanin on, kapé ready, and a chair with your name on it.

07 — Good to know

Visiting Kalan

Do you take reservations? +
Yes. Reserve a table through Messenger or by phone. Walk-ins are always welcome; reservations help on weekends and for groups of six or more.
Can I order for pickup or delivery? +
Message us to arrange pickup at the counter. Delivery is available through the usual rider apps within Marikina and nearby barangays.
Is Kalan good for groups and celebrations? +
It is. We seat small parties comfortably and can prepare lutong-bahay platters for groups with advance notice through Messenger.
Do you have vegetarian dishes? +
Several. Ask the counter for the day's vegetable and meatless options — we cook a rotating selection alongside the meat and seafood classics.
Is there parking nearby? +
Street parking is available along Bayan-bayanan Ave, with a public pay lot one block toward the plaza.
Are you family- and kid-friendly? +
Yes. Kalan is a family kitchen — high chairs are on hand and the merienda menu is a favourite with kids.
