The shop

Vulca is an auto repair shop and detailing studio on N. Bacalso Avenue in Cebu City, for drivers who're tired of being talked down to and charged for work they never agreed to.

We started as a roadside vulcanizing stall — the kind that patches your tyre at midnight — and grew into a full garage without losing the habit of just telling you straight what's wrong. Diagnose first, explain it plainly, quote it honestly, then fix it.

Two bays: one for mechanical and aircon work, one for detailing and paint care. Same crew, same standard, whether you're in for brakes or a ceramic coat.

Questions

How do I get a quote? +
Message us the make, model and what the car is doing — sounds, warning lights, when it happens. We give an honest estimate, and for anything uncertain we inspect first and confirm the price before starting work.
Do you diagnose before charging? +
Yes. We diagnose first and explain what we found, so you approve the work and the cost before we touch anything. No surprise line items at pickup.
What makes and models do you service? +
We service most common passenger cars, SUVs and pickups sold in the Philippines, petrol and diesel. Tell us your model and we'll confirm we can take it in.
Do you service car aircon? +
We do — cleaning, recharging and repair of car air-conditioning, which matters in Cebu heat. It's one of our most-requested jobs.
How long does detailing take? +
Interior and exterior detailing is usually a same-day job; paint correction and ceramic coating need the car for longer. We'll tell you the turnaround when you book.
Can I wait, or do you offer pickup? +
There's a waiting area for quick jobs. For bigger work, message us — we can arrange drop-off and pickup timing that fits your day.
