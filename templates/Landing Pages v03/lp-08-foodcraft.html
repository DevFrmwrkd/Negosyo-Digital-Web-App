Our story

The oven never really cooled

Panaderia Alba is a small-batch neighborhood bakery in Santo Rosario, San Fernando, Pampanga, baking pandesal, Kapampangan breads, and celebration cakes daily. It's for neighbors who queue at dawn, families sending pasalubong boxes, and anyone who believes bread has a right time of day.

The bakery began as Apung Alba's pugon behind the family house. When she passed the wooden peel to her grandson — a hotel pastry cook who came home — the deal was that the pandesal recipe stays, the dawn schedule stays, and only the cakes were allowed to get fancy.

The pugon is now a modern deck oven, but the starter dough still gets mixed at ten in the evening, the way it has for two generations.

Questions

Before you message

How do I order from Panaderia Alba?

Order through Messenger — send your items and pickup time and we confirm within the hour during bakery hours. Walk-ins are always welcome while trays last.

What time does pandesal come out?

The first pandesal trays come out at dawn and batches continue through mid-morning. Message us the night before to reserve for early pickup.

Do you deliver?

Yes, within San Fernando and nearby barangays via local riders — we arrange the booking for you and pack breads to travel. Cakes are best picked up.

Do you take custom cake orders?

Yes — celebration cakes and dessert trays need three days' lead time. Message us your date, theme, and size and we send options with photos.

Can I order pasalubong boxes in bulk?

Yes, we pack bread and pastry pasalubong boxes for reunions and offices. For 10 boxes or more, order two days ahead.

Are your breads baked fresh daily?

Everything is baked the same day it's sold — dough is made overnight, baked from dawn, and unsold bread goes to a feeding-program partner, never back on the shelf.
