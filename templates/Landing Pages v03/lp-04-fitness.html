The club

Built by fighters,
run for beginners

Bakal Boxing Club is a boxing and strength gym on General Maxilom (Mango) Avenue, Cebu City, running coached group classes from 6 AM to 9 PM. It's for absolute beginners, fitness boxers, and amateurs who want real corner-style coaching without competition pressure.

The club was founded by two retired amateur boxers who kept meeting office workers who "always wanted to try boxing" but were scared of fight gyms. So they built one where the fundamentals class is the main event, not a warm-up act for the fighters.

Nine years later the walls hold more first-timer hand wraps than trophies — and the coaches still count that as the better collection.

Q & A

Before the bell

I've never boxed. Can I really start here?

Yes — most members started with zero experience. Your first session is a free coached fundamentals class: stance, guard, and the first four punches, at your pace.

Do I need my own gloves and wraps?

No. Loaner gloves are free for your trial, and wraps are available at the front desk. Most members buy their own gear after a few weeks.

Will I have to spar?

No — sparring is strictly opt-in, coach-supervised, and only offered after you pass a fundamentals assessment. Many members train for years without ever sparring.

How do I claim the free trial?

Message us on Messenger with your preferred day, or walk in 15 minutes before any class. Bring water, a towel, and clothes you can move in.

Is boxing good for weight loss?

Yes — a coached boxing class burns significant calories while building coordination and strength. Consistency matters most, which is why classes run morning to night.

Are there classes for women and teens?

Yes. All group classes are mixed and beginner-friendly, and we run a dedicated women's fundamentals class and a supervised teens program on weekends.
