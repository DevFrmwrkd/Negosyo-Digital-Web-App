Shop history

From one lift to five bays

Kalye Motorworks is an independent auto repair and detailing shop in Barangay San Vicente, Biñan, Laguna, serving commuters, family cars, and small fleets along the National Highway. It's built for owners who want casa-quality work with a written estimate and no surprises.

The shop started as a single hydraulic lift and a borrowed compressor. What grew it wasn't marketing — it was the habit of photographing every worn part and texting it to the owner before touching a wrench. Fifteen years later that habit is a five-bay operation with the same policy.

Every mechanic on the floor is TESDA-certified, and the head diagnostician has been reading engine codes since OBD-II was new money.

FAQ

Straight answers

Do you give free estimates?

Yes — diagnosis and written estimates are free. We inspect, list the work and parts, and you approve before a single bolt turns.

Can I wait while my car is serviced?

Yes, there's an air-conditioned waiting area with Wi-Fi. Most PMS jobs finish within two to three hours.

Do you use original or replacement parts?

Your choice — we quote both OEM and quality replacement options and explain the trade-offs. We never install a part you didn't approve.

Do you service vehicles still under casa warranty?

Yes. Preventive maintenance outside the casa doesn't void your warranty, and we keep full records you can present.

Do you offer home or roadside service?

Yes, for batteries, jump starts, and minor electrical issues within Biñan, Santa Rosa, and San Pedro. Call and we dispatch a mobile mechanic.

How do I get a quote without visiting?

Send photos or a video of the issue plus your car's year and model over Messenger or Viber — ballpark quote the same day.
