Company record

Two toolboxes and a promise

Linya Aircon & Electrical is a service-area business covering Quezon City and five neighboring cities, handling aircon cleaning and repair, electrical troubleshooting, and safe installations for homes and small offices. It's for households that want a licensed crew, a written quote, and a technician who shows up inside the promised window.

The company started with two brothers — one a licensed electrician, one a TESDA RAC technician — sharing a motorcycle and a phone number. The promise that built the business: quote first, work second, and never leave a panel or a unit messier than we found it.

Now there are four uniformed crews and a dispatcher, but the phone number — and the promise — are still the same.

Scope of work

Services

A-01 · Aircon cleaning

General cleaning for window and split-type units — coils, blower, drain line, and a cooling check after.

A-02 · Aircon repair & freon

No-cooling diagnosis, leak repair, freon top-up, capacitor and fan-motor replacement.

A-03 · Install & relocation

New split-type installation and unit transfers with proper piping, vacuuming, and bracketing.

E-01 · Electrical troubleshooting

Tripping breakers, dead outlets, flickering lines — traced and fixed with a written finding.

E-02 · Wiring & panel work

Rewiring, panel upgrades, grounding, and load balancing supervised by a licensed electrician.

E-03 · Fixture installation

Lighting, ceiling fans, water heaters, EV outlets — mounted, wired, and tested to spec.

Q & A

Frequently asked

Do you charge for the site visit?

No — inspection and quotation are free within our service area. You only pay if you approve the quote and we do the work.

How fast can a technician arrive?

Same-day dispatch is usually possible for calls before noon; otherwise next morning. For no-cooling aircon and power-outage emergencies we prioritize the queue.

Are your technicians licensed?

Yes — electrical work is handled or supervised by a licensed electrician, and all technicians are TESDA-certified in RAC servicing or electrical installation.

Do you service both split-type and window aircon?

Yes — window, split-type, and light-commercial cassette units. Cleaning, freon top-up, leak repair, relocation, and new installation.

Is there a warranty on the work?

Yes — workmanship is covered by a written service warranty stated on your receipt, and we return free of charge if the same issue recurs within the warranty period.

How do I get a quote?

Message photos of your unit or panel plus your barangay on Messenger or Viber, and we send a quotation the same day. Confirmed jobs get a scheduled dispatch window.
